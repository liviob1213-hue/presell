How your Life Path Number is calculated

In numerology, the Life Path Number comes from your full date of birth. It is the most commonly used number in a numerology chart, because your birth date never changes.

  1. Reduce the month to a single digit.
  2. Reduce the day to a single digit.
  3. Reduce the year by adding its digits until you get a single digit.
  4. Add the three results and reduce again until you reach a single digit.

If a step lands on 11, 22 or 33, many numerologists keep it as it is. These are called master numbers.

Example: March 14, 1985
Month: 3 → 3
Day: 14 → 1 + 4 = 5
Year: 1985 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 5 = 23 → 2 + 3 = 5
Total: 3 + 5 + 5 = 13 → 1 + 3 = Life Path 4

Different numerology schools reduce the date in slightly different ways, so two calculators can occasionally disagree, especially around master numbers. This calculator uses the month–day–year method shown above.

What each Life Path Number means

These are the themes numerology traditionally associates with each number. Read them as prompts for reflection, not predictions.

1The Initiator

Independent and driven, with a natural pull toward starting things and leading. The growth edge is learning to accept help and share the spotlight.

2The Peacemaker

Sensitive to people and atmosphere, good at cooperation and seeing both sides. The growth edge is speaking up for your own needs.

3The Communicator

Expressive, social and creative, often drawn to words, art or performance. The growth edge is focus and finishing what you start.

4The Builder

Practical, loyal and steady, happiest with a plan and a solid foundation. The growth edge is flexibility when plans change.

5The Explorer

Curious and freedom-loving, energized by change, travel and new experiences. The growth edge is commitment and consistency.

6The Nurturer

Caring and responsible, often the one others lean on at home or work. The growth edge is caring for yourself as well as others.

7The Seeker

Thoughtful and introspective, drawn to questions, research and inner life. The growth edge is trusting others and staying connected.

8The Achiever

Ambitious and capable, with a strong sense for goals, money and structure. The growth edge is balancing achievement with rest and relationships.

9The Humanitarian

Compassionate and idealistic, with a wide view of the world and other people. The growth edge is letting go of the past and setting limits.

11The Intuitive

A master number linked to intuition, sensitivity and inspiring others. It is often described as intense, with a growth edge around managing nervous energy.

22The Master Builder

A master number linked to turning big visions into something real and lasting. The growth edge is handling the pressure of high expectations.

33The Master Teacher

A rare master number associated with service, compassion and guiding others. The growth edge is not carrying everyone else's weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Life Path Number?

It is a single number (1–9, or the master numbers 11, 22 and 33) calculated from your date of birth. In numerology it is treated as the main theme of your chart.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. You can find your number and read its meaning without paying or signing up.

Why did another calculator give me a different number?

Some calculators add all the digits of the date at once, while others reduce the month, day and year separately. The difference usually shows up with master numbers. This page uses the month–day–year method.

What are master numbers?

11, 22 and 33 are called master numbers. Many numerologists do not reduce them further and describe them as more intense versions of 2, 4 and 6.

Can numerology predict my future?

No. Numerology is a tradition people use for self-reflection and fun. It is not science, and it should not replace professional advice on health, money or relationships.
